Thread rules
  • Post 0 — introduction. Introduce yourself. No paper needed. Must include an eye-catching image or movie.
  • Paper posts. Your story about the paper, plus the paper link. At least 4 research articles (preprints count) and 1 review.
  • Context / commentary posts. Background, history, a transition between papers, an open question. No paper needed — use as many as you like.
  • Closing post(s). Summary, personal opinion, open questions, or thanks. Link the labs mentioned, e.g. @epimechfc.bsky.social.
  • Length: 275 characters including spaces for your text. Paper links go in their own box and are not counted against it.
  • Media: required for Post 0 only. Optional but very welcome on the rest. Images JPEG/PNG, max 1 MB. Movies MOV/MP4. Name them Post0, Post1, … to match the post numbers.

Your files are checked in your browser only — nothing is uploaded from this page.
